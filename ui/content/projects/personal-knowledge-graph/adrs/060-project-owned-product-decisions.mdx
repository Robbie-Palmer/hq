## Context

[ADR 059](/projects/personal-knowledge-graph/adrs/059-product-decision-records)
introduced Product Decision Records with global identities and storage. Owner
review of the first record established that product decisions belong to a
project, just as architecture decisions do. Shared policy has an owner too:
operational evidence requirements belong to Personal Engineering Platform,
while Personal Knowledge Graph publishes them.

## Decision

Store PDRs in `ui/content/projects/<project>/pdrs/`. Number them monotonically
within their owning project, never reusing a number. Derive ownership from the
directory and use `<project>:<number>-<slug>` as the canonical reference. Two
projects may use the same local number and slug without sharing an identity.

Publish each record at `/projects/<project>/pdrs/<slug>` with a Markdown twin,
project breadcrumb, and a link from the project's product decisions tab.
`/product-decisions` remains a cross-project index, not an ownership boundary.
Reject standalone files in the former global directory.

Retain the earlier distinction between product intent and architecture, the
lifecycle dates and immutable history, evidence links, idea links, and directional
ADR relations. `affected_projects` is optional and names additional consumers;
it does not establish ownership. `supersedes`, `implements_product_decisions`,
and blog `product_decisions` use qualified PDR references. Accepted successors
derive the earlier record's Superseded status without changing its file.

## Consequences

Project pages own their product and architecture histories. Shared choices have
one owner and explicit consumers. References, graph keys, routes, and generated
Markdown must retain the project qualifier. No global PDR has been published,
so there is no published detail URL to migrate.
