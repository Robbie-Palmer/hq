# The family recipe book should learn from every meal

Recipe Site turns cooking, adapting, and repeating recipes into useful evidence for the people you trust.

<PitchNotes>
  This began as a digital replacement for our handwritten recipe book. Search, editing, scaling, and backup solve
  real problems, but they do not justify another recipe manager. The larger idea is a social cooking network built
  from what reaches the table.

    \[/Sources]
</PitchNotes>

<p className="pitch-slide__statement">Paper preserves the recipe. It loses what happened next.</p>

<PitchNotes>
  The physical book is good at being glanceable and hard to interrupt. It is bad at capture, search, safe edits,
  portion changes, measurement conversion, shopping, and backup. Most of all, it cannot connect a recipe to the
  changes and repeated cooks that prove it works.

<PitchNotes>
  A star rating collapses many kinds of value. A completed cook is stronger evidence than a view, and a repeat is
  stronger again. These events need precise definitions. Starting cook mode is not the same as completing a meal,
  and a repeat should stay tied to the exact recipe version that reached the table.

## One score hides two different questions

<div className="pitch-taste-map">
  <span className="pitch-taste-map__axis pitch-taste-map__axis--taste">How good was it? →</span>
  <span className="pitch-taste-map__axis pitch-taste-map__axis--fit">How well did it fit today? →</span>
  <div className="is-fallback"><strong>Emergency fallback</strong><small>useful · not a favourite</small></div>
  <div className="is-staple"><strong>Tuesday staple</strong><small>good · fast · reliable</small></div>
  <div className="is-skip"><strong>Skip next time</strong><small>neither enjoyable nor useful</small></div>
  <div className="is-treat"><strong>Worth the work?</strong><small>great result · wrong occasion</small></div>
</div>

<PitchNotes>
  Enjoyment and practical fit are related but distinct. A spectacular dish may appear once a year. A good meal can
  become foundational because it is predictable, affordable, accepted by the household, and compatible with the
  time and energy available. Ranking should preserve both truths.

## A recommendation should arrive with evidence

<div className="pitch-recommendation-card">
  <div className="pitch-recommendation-card__sender"><span>E</span><p><strong>Ellie recommended this</strong><small>"the kids actually ate this, zero complaints."</small></p></div>
  <div className="pitch-recommendation-card__recipe"><span>weeknight · 25 min</span><strong>Chicken quesadillas</strong><p>Made <b>128×</b> · <b>54%</b> made it again</p></div>
  <div className="pitch-recommendation-card__actions"><span>Save to box</span><span>Add to plan</span><strong>Cook now</strong></div>
</div>

<p className="pitch-slide__statement">Personal context, public proof, and an action that closes the loop.</p>

<PitchNotes>
  This card comes from the mid-fi design language. A deliberate person-to-person hand-off stays distinct from an
  algorithmic recommendation. The note explains why Ellie chose it. Public cook and repeat signals supply broader
  evidence. Save, plan, and cook actions let the product observe whether the hand-off helped.

  * [Product design](/projects/recipe-site?tab=overview#product-design)

## The network grows from ordinary cooking

<div className="pitch-cooking-flywheel">
  <div><span>1</span><strong>Better fit</strong><small>A useful recipe reaches the right kitchen</small></div>
  <i>→</i>
  <div><span>2</span><strong>More cooking</strong><small>The household plans, shops, and cooks</small></div>
  <i>→</i>
  <div><span>3</span><strong>Better evidence</strong><small>Repeats and adaptations keep their lineage</small></div>
  <i className="pitch-cooking-flywheel__repeat">repeat</i>
</div>

<p className="pitch-slide__statement">No posting streak. No engagement chore. Dinner is the contribution.</p>

<PitchNotes>
  The public network should grow from useful personal actions. People do not need to write a review after every
  meal. When they choose to share a cook, repeat, adaptation, or recommendation outcome, the graph becomes more
  useful. Visibility stays explicit: personal, household, connections, or public.

## The build has an honest stop condition

<PitchColumns>
  <PitchColumn>
    ### Use Tandoor

    If the real job is collection management, migration, planning, and self-hosted shared shopping.
  </PitchColumn>

  <PitchColumn>
    ### Keep building

    Only if cooks, lineage, trust, and kitchen context produce better next-meal decisions.
  </PitchColumn>
</PitchColumns>

<PitchStep>
  If the network does not help real friends and families cook better, migrate the collection.
</PitchStep>

<PitchNotes>
  Tandoor is mature and far ahead in collection administration. Recipe Site earns its maintenance cost only if
  the social learning loop becomes useful. The test is behavioural: did a trusted recommendation get planned,
  cooked, enjoyed, adapted, or repeated?
