## Context

[PDR 003](/projects/pathologize/pdrs/003-independent-pathology-benchmark-families)
separates quality, tiling, tissue, cell, representation, and slide-level
benchmarks. Those families still ask questions at different evidence levels.
A cell location, tissue boundary, slide grade, and patient outcome do not share
one label source, observation unit, or useful error measure.

Flattening those questions into one score would discard location, boundaries,
counts, continuous values, follow-up time, or the distinction between observed
and inferred evidence. It would also let the available model method define the
question instead of responding to it.

## Proposed decision

Define each benchmark around the user question and the evidence unit that can
answer it. Every task contract must state the observation unit, target,
prediction record, label source and timepoint, eligibility, leakage unit,
uncertainty, metrics, failure slices, resource limits, and intended consumer.
It must preserve the native shape of evidence when location, boundaries,
counts, order, continuous values, or time-to-event outcomes matter.

A slide or patient product may consume pinned cell, tissue-structure, or slide
evidence. That composition does not turn an upstream model output into ground
truth or assign a slide or patient label to every contributing region. The
benchmark must retain the lower-level record identities and expose unsupported
or missing evidence.

Compare candidates through Pathologize's fixed development, test, and external
evaluation products. Keep final test and external cohorts sealed. Model family,
feature aggregation, training framework, storage, and execution mechanics are
architecture choices. A paper or commercial system can supply target evidence
or a candidate method without defining the product contract.

## Consequences

Pathologize can change technical methods without changing what a benchmark asks
or how its evidence is judged. Weak slide or patient labels remain weak labels
rather than fabricated region labels.

The project must maintain several prediction schemas, metrics, and evidence
contracts. Cross-task comparisons need explicit dependency versions and cannot
collapse into one leaderboard score. A candidate that produces several useful
outputs may need evaluation in more than one contract.

## Evaluation

Accept the proposal when cell, tissue-structure, slide, and patient questions
can each publish native prediction records, resource measurements, failure
slices, label provenance, and evidence-level metrics from pinned Pathologize
evaluation products. Reject it if a model method, framework, dataset layout,
storage choice, or native score becomes the product definition.
