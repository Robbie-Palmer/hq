## Goal

Reduce the cost, delay, and specialist effort needed to match patients with appropriate tests and treatments.

## Scope

The computational pathology projects extract signals and usable tissue regions from whole slide images. The bioinformatics platform gives researchers a shared environment for combining those results with genomic and other omic data.

These projects took different routes, but each made biological or clinical data more useful for patient-specific decisions.
