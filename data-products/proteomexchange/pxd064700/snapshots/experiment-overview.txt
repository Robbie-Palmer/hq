Proteomic profiles from eight post-mortem donors (64–101 y) were compared after three preservation strategies—fresh-frozen, formalin-fixed, and paraffin-embedded (FFPE). For each donor three independent tissue pieces (biological replicates b1–b3) were taken from hippocampus and prefrontal cortex; every biological replicate was further split into two technical replicates at the protein-extraction step. All digests were analyzed by data-independent LC-HRMS (69 × 15 m/z windows). The resulting dataset enables assessment of fixation-induced biases and provides quantitative protein lists for low- versus high-Braak Alzheimer stages.
