# Data licence and attribution

The HM Revenue & Customs source material and adapted rule values in this package
contain public sector information licensed under the [Open Government Licence
v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).

Retain this attribution when copying or distributing the source extracts or rule
artifacts:

> © Crown copyright. Contains public sector information from HM Revenue & Customs
> licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Each generated rule also records its source title, publisher, URL, publication date,
retrieval date, and content checksum. Keep that provenance with redistributed data
where practical. The Open Government Licence does not cover third-party material
identified by a source publication, departmental logos, or personal data.

The TypeScript code, schemas, tests, and build scripts remain under the repository's
software licence.
