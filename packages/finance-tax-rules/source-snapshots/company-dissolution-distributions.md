A distribution made in anticipation of striking off may receive capital
treatment under CTA 2010 section 1030A when the company has secured or intends
to secure amounts due to it, has satisfied or intends to satisfy its debts, and
the total distributions do not exceed £25,000. If the company is not dissolved
within two years, or the debt condition is not met, normal distribution
treatment applies.

A members' voluntary liquidation applies to a solvent company that can pay its
debts within 12 months. A licensed insolvency practitioner acts as liquidator,
and distributions made during the winding up are normally capital
distributions.

Anti-avoidance rules can instead treat a winding-up distribution as income,
including cases where the recipient continues the same or a similar trade.
This calculation does not decide anti-avoidance, insolvency, or legal
eligibility. Any unresolved case remains unsupported.
