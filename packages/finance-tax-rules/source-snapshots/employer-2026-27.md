# HMRC employer rates 2026/27 extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Published: 2026-01-30
- Retrieved: 2026-10-01

The standard Personal Allowance is £12,570. England, Northern Ireland, and Wales
use a £37,700 basic-rate band, with rates of 20%, 40%, and 45% and an
additional-rate threshold of £125,140. Scotland uses cumulative band limits of
£3,967, £16,956, £31,092, £62,430, and £125,140 at 19%, 20%, 21%, 42%, and 45%,
followed by 48%.

For Class 1 category A, the weekly/monthly Lower Earnings Limit, Primary Threshold,
and Upper Earnings Limit are £129/£559, £242/£1,048, and £967/£4,189. Employee
rates are 8% and 2%.
