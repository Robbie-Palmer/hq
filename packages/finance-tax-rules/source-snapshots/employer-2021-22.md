# HMRC employer rates 2021/22 extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2021-to-2022>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Published: 2021-02-02
- Retrieved: 2026-10-05

The standard Personal Allowance was £12,570. England, Northern Ireland, and
Wales used a £37,700 taxable basic-rate band at 20%, then 40% through £150,000
and 45% above it. Scotland used taxable bands of £2,097, £10,629, £18,366, and
£118,908 at 19%, 20%, 21%, and 41%, followed by 46%.

For Class 1 category A, the weekly/monthly Lower Earnings Limit was £120/£520,
the Primary Threshold was £184/£797, and the Upper Earnings Limit was
£967/£4,189. Employee rates were 12% and 2%.
