# HMRC employer rates 2017/18 extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2017-to-2018>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Published: 2017-02-09
- Retrieved: 2026-10-05

The standard Personal Allowance was £11,500. The UK taxable basic-rate band was
£33,500; Scotland used £31,500. Both schedules then applied 40% through
£150,000 and 45% above £150,000.

For Class 1 category A, the weekly/monthly Lower Earnings Limit was £113/£490,
the Primary Threshold was £157/£680, and the Upper Earnings Limit was
£866/£3,750. Employee rates were 12% and 2%.
