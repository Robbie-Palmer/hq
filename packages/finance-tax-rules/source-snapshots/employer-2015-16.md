The standard Personal Allowance was £10,600. The taxable basic-rate band was
£31,785 at 20%, followed by 40% through £150,000 and 45% above £150,000.

For Class 1 category A, the weekly/monthly Lower Earnings Limit was £112/£486,
the Primary Threshold was £155/£672, and the Upper Earnings Limit was
£815/£3,532. Employee rates were 12% between the Primary Threshold and Upper
Earnings Limit and 2% above it.
