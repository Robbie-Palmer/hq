# GOV.UK workplace pension methods extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/workplace-pensions/managing-your-pension>
- Publisher: GOV.UK
- Retrieved: 2026-10-02

With net pay, the employer deducts the member contribution before calculating
Income Tax. The payslip contribution includes the tax relief. A worker who does not
pay Income Tax receives no tax relief through this method.

With relief at source, the employer deducts the member payment after Income Tax and
National Insurance. The pension provider adds basic-rate relief to the pension pot.
The payslip shows the member payment, not the provider relief. People who pay above
the basic rate may need to claim further relief.
