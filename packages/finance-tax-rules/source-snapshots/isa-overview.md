# HMRC Individual Savings Accounts overview extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/individual-savings-accounts/overview>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Retrieved: 2026-10-04
- Coverage used: 2025/26 and 2026/27

The annual ISA subscription limit is £20,000 in both supported tax years.
Interest, income, and capital gains inside an ISA are tax-free. An ISA cannot
be held jointly.

The calculator checks each household member's recorded subscriptions against
the annual limit. It excludes income and disposals linked to ISA accounts from
the taxable totals.
