# HMRC net-pay extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm044230>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Published: 2015-03-27
- Retrieved: 2026-10-01

Under a net-pay arrangement, payroll deducts the member contribution from gross
pay before calculating Income Tax. The contribution does not reduce earnings for
National Insurance. The payroll deduction provides full Income Tax relief at the
member's marginal rate.
