# GOV.UK tax on savings interest extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/apply-tax-free-interest-on-savings/how-much-is-tax-free>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Published: 2012-01-25
- Updated: 2016-04-06
- Retrieved: 2026-10-04

Unused Personal Allowance applies to savings interest after non-savings income.
The starting rate for savings provides a zero-percent band of up to £5,000. Each
£1 of non-savings income above the Personal Allowance reduces that band by £1.

The Personal Savings Allowance is £1,000 for a basic-rate taxpayer, £500 for a
higher-rate taxpayer, and nil for an additional-rate taxpayer. Savings income is
included when determining the taxpayer's highest Income Tax band. Interest above
the available allowances is taxed at the applicable savings rate.

Interest and other income within an ISA does not use the Personal Savings
Allowance because the wrapper already excludes it from taxable savings income.
