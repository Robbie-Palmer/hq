# HMRC pension scheme rates extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowances-pension-schemes/pension-schemes-rates>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Updated: 2026-04-06
- Retrieved: 2026-10-01

Tax relief on member contributions is limited to the higher of 100% of relevant UK
earnings or £3,600. The 2015/16 transitional annual allowance was £80,000 from
6 April through 8 July and zero from 9 July, with up to £40,000 of unused
pre-alignment allowance available after the split. The standard annual allowance
was £40,000 from 2016/17 through 2022/23 and £60,000 from 2023/24.

Tapering began in 2016/17. Its threshold-income and adjusted-income limits were
£110,000 and £150,000 through 2019/20, then £200,000 and £240,000 through
2022/23. The adjusted-income limit became £260,000 in 2023/24. The minimum
tapered allowance was £10,000 through 2019/20, £4,000 from 2020/21 through
2022/23, and £10,000 from 2023/24.

The money purchase annual allowance follows the 2015/16 transitional split at
£20,000 then zero, was £10,000 in 2016/17, £4,000 from 2017/18 through 2022/23,
and £10,000 from 2023/24.
