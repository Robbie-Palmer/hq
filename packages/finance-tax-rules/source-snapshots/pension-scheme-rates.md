# HMRC pension scheme rates extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rates-and-allowances-pension-schemes/pension-schemes-rates>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Updated: 2026-04-06
- Retrieved: 2026-10-01

Tax relief on member contributions is limited to the higher of 100% of relevant UK
earnings or £3,600. The annual allowance was £40,000 in 2022/23 and £60,000 from
2023/24 through 2026/27.

The threshold-income limit was £200,000 throughout this dataset. The
adjusted-income limit was £240,000 in 2022/23 and £260,000 from 2023/24. The
minimum tapered annual allowance and money purchase annual allowance were £4,000
in 2022/23 and £10,000 from 2023/24.
