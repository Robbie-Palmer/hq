# HMRC relief-at-source extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/pensions-tax-manual/ptm044220>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Published: 2015-03-27
- Retrieved: 2026-10-01

Under relief at source, the member pays a net contribution and the scheme
administrator claims relief at the relevant basic rate. The relief and member
payment form the gross contribution. Members liable above the relevant basic rate
may need to claim further relief from HMRC. This method does not reduce pay used
for payroll Income Tax or National Insurance.
