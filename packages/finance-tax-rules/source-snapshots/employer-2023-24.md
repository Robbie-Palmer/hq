# HMRC employer rates 2023/24 extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2023-to-2024>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Published: 2023-02-27
- Retrieved: 2026-10-01

The standard Personal Allowance was £12,570. England, Northern Ireland, and Wales
used a £37,700 basic-rate band, with rates of 20%, 40%, and 45% and an
additional-rate threshold of £125,140. Scotland used cumulative band limits of
£2,162, £13,118, £31,092, and £125,140 at 19%, 20%, 21%, and 42%, followed by 47%.

For Class 1 category A, the weekly/monthly Lower Earnings Limit, Primary Threshold,
and Upper Earnings Limit were £123/£533, £242/£1,048, and £967/£4,189. Employee
rates were 12% and 2% through 5 January 2024, then 10% and 2% from 6 January.
