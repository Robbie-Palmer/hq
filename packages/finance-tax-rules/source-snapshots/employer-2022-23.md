The standard Personal Allowance was £12,570. England, Northern Ireland, and Wales
used 20% on the first £37,700 above the allowance, 40% through £150,000, then 45%.
Scotland used cumulative band limits of £2,162, £13,118, £31,092, and £150,000 at
19%, 20%, 21%, and 41%, followed by 46%.

For Class 1 category A, the weekly/monthly Lower Earnings Limit was £123/£533 and
the Upper Earnings Limit was £967/£4,189. The Primary Threshold was £190/£823 from
6 April through 5 July, then £242/£1,048. Employee rates were 13.25% and 3.25%
through 5 November, then 12% and 2% from 6 November.
