# HMRC employer rates 2019/20 extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2019-to-2020>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Published: 2019-01-11
- Retrieved: 2026-10-05

The standard Personal Allowance was £12,500. England, Northern Ireland, and
Wales used a £37,500 taxable basic-rate band at 20%, then 40% through £150,000
and 45% above it. Scotland used taxable bands of £2,049, £10,395, £18,486, and
£119,070 at 19%, 20%, 21%, and 41%, followed by 46%.

For Class 1 category A, the weekly/monthly Lower Earnings Limit was £118/£512,
the Primary Threshold was £166/£719, and the Upper Earnings Limit was
£962/£4,167. Employee rates were 12% and 2%.
