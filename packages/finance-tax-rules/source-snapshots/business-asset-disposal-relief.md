# HMRC Business Asset Disposal Relief extract

- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Retrieved: 2026-11-07
- Coverage used: 2025/26 and 2026/27

For a disposal of non-EMI shares in a personal company, the individual must
have been an officer or employee and the company must have been a trading
company throughout the two-year qualifying period. The individual must hold at
least 5% of the ordinary share capital and voting rights. The holding must also
give at least a 5% economic entitlement through profits and assets on a winding
up, or through company-sale proceeds.

Qualifying gains are subject to a £1 million lifetime limit for the supported
tax years. The rate is 14% for disposals from 6 April 2025 through 5 April 2026
and 18% from 6 April 2026. Gains above the remaining lifetime limit use the
ordinary Capital Gains Tax rates.

The supported calculation requires the user to record each eligibility fact,
prior lifetime qualifying gains, available losses, remaining annual exempt
amount, and remaining Income Tax bands. EMI shares, dilution elections,
associated disposals, trusts, and unverified eligibility remain unsupported.
