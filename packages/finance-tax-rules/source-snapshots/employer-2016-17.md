# HMRC employer rates 2016/17 extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2016-to-2017>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Published: 2016-02-04
- Retrieved: 2026-10-05

The standard Personal Allowance was £11,000. The same schedule applied in
Scotland and the rest of the UK: 20% on the first £32,000 above the allowance,
40% through £150,000, then 45%.

For Class 1 category A, the weekly/monthly Lower Earnings Limit was £112/£486,
the Primary Threshold was £155/£672, and the Upper Earnings Limit was
£827/£3,583. Employee rates were 12% and 2%.
