# Automatic enrolment qualifying-earnings bands extract

- GOV.UK collection: <https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/automatic-enrolment-earnings-trigger-and-qualifying-earnings-band>
- Department for Work and Pensions annual reviews published from 17 December 2014 through 18 December 2025
- Pay-period table: <https://www.thepensionsregulator.gov.uk/business-advisers/automatic-enrolment-guide-for-business-advisers/automatic-enrolment-earnings-threshold>
- Retrieved: 2026-10-29

The qualifying-earnings band is the portion of earnings on which automatic-enrolment
minimum contributions apply. The annual Department for Work and Pensions reviews set
the lower and upper limits. The Pensions Regulator publishes the statutory rounded
amounts for each pay reference period.

The reviewed annual lower and upper limits are:

| Tax year | Lower limit | Upper limit |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2015/16 | £5,824 | £42,385 |
| 2016/17 | £5,824 | £43,000 |
| 2017/18 | £5,876 | £45,000 |
| 2018/19 | £6,032 | £46,350 |
| 2019/20 | £6,136 | £50,000 |
| 2020/21 | £6,240 | £50,000 |
| 2021/22 to 2026/27 | £6,240 | £50,270 |

For 2026/27 the rounded lower limits for one week, two weeks, four weeks, one
month, three months, six months, and one year are £120, £240, £480, £520,
£1,560, £3,120, and £6,240. The corresponding upper limits are £967, £1,934,
£3,867, £4,189, £12,568, £25,135, and £50,270. The checked-in rules preserve
the tables for each supported year, including historical rounding differences.

Qualifying earnings include salary or wages, commission, bonuses, overtime,
Statutory Sick Pay, and statutory maternity, paternity, adoption, and shared
parental pay. Statutory parental bereavement pay applies from 2020/21, and
statutory neonatal care pay applies from 2025/26. A scheme can define a
different pensionable-pay basis, so the statutory band must not be treated as a
universal scheme rule.
