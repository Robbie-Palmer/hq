# HMRC salary-sacrifice extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/salary-sacrifice-and-the-effects-on-paye>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Published: 2014-06-12
- Retrieved: 2026-10-01

A salary-sacrifice arrangement changes the employment contract and reduces the
employee's entitlement to cash pay. HMRC's pension example exchanges a £5,000
bonus for a £5,000 employer pension contribution, with no employment Income Tax or
employee National Insurance charge on the sacrificed amount. Employer and member
contributions must remain distinct in downstream calculations.
