# HMRC employer rates 2018/19 extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2018-to-2019>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Published: 2018-01-04
- Retrieved: 2026-10-05

The standard Personal Allowance was £11,850. The UK schedule used a £34,500
taxable basic-rate band at 20%, then 40% through £150,000 and 45% above it.
Scotland used taxable bands of £2,000, £10,150, £19,430, and £118,420 at 19%,
20%, 21%, and 41%, followed by 46%.

For Class 1 category A, the weekly/monthly Lower Earnings Limit was £116/£503,
the Primary Threshold was £162/£702, and the Upper Earnings Limit was
£892/£3,863. Employee rates were 12% and 2%.
