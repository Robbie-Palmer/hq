# HMRC Capital Gains Tax rates and allowances extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/capital-gains-tax-rates-and-allowances>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Retrieved: 2026-10-04
- Coverage used: 2025/26 and 2026/27

For individuals, the annual exempt amount is £3,000 in both supported tax
years. Gains above that amount use an 18% rate within the unused Income Tax
basic-rate band and a 24% rate above it. The same rates apply to residential
property gains in these years.

The calculator does not apply reliefs, elections, deferred gains, or special
rates. It reports those inputs as unsupported instead.
