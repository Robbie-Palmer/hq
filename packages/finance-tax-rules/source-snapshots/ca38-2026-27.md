# HMRC CA38 2026/27 extract

- Source: <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ca38-national-insurance-contributions-tables-a-and-j>
- Publisher: HM Revenue & Customs
- Published: 2026-04-02
- Retrieved: 2026-10-02

CA38 provides separate weekly and monthly tables and permits the exact-percentage
method. For employee category A from 6 April 2026, the weekly Primary Threshold is
£242 and the Upper Earnings Limit is £967. The monthly values are £1,048 and
£4,189. Employee contributions are 8% between those thresholds and 2% above the
Upper Earnings Limit.

The manual tables use stepped earnings values, so their result can differ slightly
from exact-percentage payroll. The validation fixtures use the exact-percentage
method and choose amounts that do not create a half-penny rounding tie.
